TRAVEL DIARY · DENMARK

Copenhagen – A Free Chicken Roll with Consequences

A spontaneous Scandinavian adventure, freezing temperatures, missed landmarks and one little airline snack that came with a very long-lasting surprise.

📍 Copenhagen, Denmark ❄️ February 2026 ✍️ Personal travel story
04

THE STORY BEGINS

A spontaneous flight and a very questionable snack

In early February 2026, I spontaneously hopped on a plane to Copenhagen. Did I check the weather forecast beforehand? Absolutely not. Were the cheap flights tempting enough to make me book the trip anyway? Quite possibly!

The plan was to explore Denmark and get a first taste of Scandinavia. What I hadn't anticipated was just how cold it would be, how many famous sights I'd end up skipping and how memorable a tiny airline snack would become.

“

I went looking for a Scandinavian adventure. I came home with frozen limbs, unfinished sightseeing plans and three weeks of digestive misery. All thanks to a 50-gram chicken roll?

01

THE SCANDINAVIAN ADVENTURE

First stop: Sweden, then straight to Denmark

After landing at the airport in Denmark, I didn't head straight into Copenhagen. Instead, I made my way to Malmö in Sweden first. How that little detour went and what I got up to there is a story of its own, which you can find in my Malmö post on the Sweden page.

From Malmö, I travelled across the famous Øresund Bridge and straight into the centre of Copenhagen. Crossing between two countries on a single journey was a pretty memorable way to continue my trip.

There was just one problem: the weather. After experiencing the freezing cold in Sweden, I was expecting much the same in Denmark. And, unfortunately, I wasn't disappointed.

The occasional ray of sunlight reflecting off the street became my personal survival strategy. I genuinely found myself switching sides of the road every couple of minutes just to catch a little more sunshine and avoid freezing to death.

Those precious rays of sunlight made the temperatures slightly more bearable, but they certainly didn't turn Copenhagen into a winter wonderland I wanted to explore outdoors for hours on end.

02

SURVIVAL MODE

When sightseeing turns into shop-hopping

It didn't take long for the cold to become unbearable. My solution? Spend as much time as possible inside shops throughout the city. After all, if the weather wasn't going to cooperate, I might as well take advantage of every opportunity to warm up.

Despite the temperatures, I still tried to make the most of my visit and see as much of Copenhagen as possible. I wanted to experience the city's culture, explore its streets and tick off a few of its famous landmarks.

Unfortunately, two well-known sights didn't make the cut. The world-famous Little Mermaid statue was simply too far away from the train station for me to justify the journey in those temperatures.

Nyhavn, with its colourful waterfront houses, also had to wait for another visit. At that point, looking after my health was a higher priority than squeezing every last sight into my itinerary.

And by "looking after my health", I mean avoiding the very real possibility of losing feeling in my limbs while trying to admire some pretty buildings.

✦

Travel tip: if your sightseeing strategy involves hopping between shops to stay alive, perhaps checking the weather forecast beforehand wouldn't hurt.

I did my best to make the most of the day, but the cold definitely limited what I was willing to do. My first Scandinavian trip wasn't quite the experience I'd hoped for.

03

THE AFTERMATH

A free chicken roll with very expensive consequences

Now, you might think the freezing temperatures were the biggest problem of this trip. But no. The real trouble started while I was waiting for my train to Sweden.

I'd been given a snack by the airline I'd flown with, and I decided to eat it while waiting for my train. Nothing fancy, just a small chicken roll weighing around 50 grams. Free food? Why not!

At the time, I thought absolutely nothing of it. It was just a little snack to keep me going. I certainly didn't expect it to become the most memorable part of the entire trip.

Soon afterwards, however, I developed severe diarrhoea that continued for three whole weeks. And when I say three weeks, I mean three weeks of watery, extremely runny diarrhoea.

Yay. Just what every traveller dreams of.

Things got so bad that I nearly had several accidents because the urge came on so suddenly that I sometimes struggled to reach the nearest toilet in time. It wasn't exactly a pleasant experience, to put it mildly.

So why am I sharing such an unglamorous detail on a travel website? Because it was genuinely unusual for me. My body can usually handle quite a lot while travelling, and I rarely get sick, especially with stomach problems.

This time, though, the symptoms were extreme and lasted far longer than I would ever have expected from such an ordinary day. The timing made that little chicken roll a rather suspicious suspect, although I can't know for certain that it was the cause.

✦

The snack was free. The consequences certainly weren't. Sometimes, you really do get what you pay for.

MY FINAL THOUGHTS

Would I give Copenhagen another chance?

My first trip to Scandinavia was, overall, a bit of a disappointment. The freezing temperatures made sightseeing difficult, and I missed out on some of Copenhagen's most famous attractions because I simply couldn't face spending any more time out in the cold.

The stomach problems that followed certainly didn't improve the memory of the trip. Three weeks of feeling unwell because of something I ate while travelling was hardly the souvenir I had in mind.

Still, a disappointing first visit has never put me off returning to a country. There's always another season, another opportunity and another chance to experience a place under better circumstances.

So, Denmark, don't worry. I'll be back. Next time, I'll check the weather forecast, dress accordingly and hopefully avoid any suspicious airline snacks.

✦
THE ONE DAY EXPLORER VERDICT

Copenhagen deserves another chance, preferably in warmer weather. The sightseeing was cut short by the cold, and the aftermath of one little snack made this trip unforgettable for all the wrong reasons.
