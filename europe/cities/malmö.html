TRAVEL DIARY · SWEDEN

Malmö – Didn't Freeze to Death, Thanks to a Random Company

Cancelled trains, temperatures that felt like a personal attack, and an unexpected corporate refuge that saved my Scandinavian adventure.

📍 Malmö, Sweden ❄️ February 2026 ✍️ Personal travel story
05

THE STORY BEGINS

A spontaneous detour into the Swedish deep freeze

Looking back, I sometimes wonder what I was thinking. In early February 2026, I spontaneously flew to Copenhagen and decided to visit Malmö as well. What had originally been a road trip through Denmark, Sweden, Norway, and Finland had somehow turned into a flying day trip — probably because the flights were cheap. Checking the weather beforehand? Apparently, that wasn't part of the plan.

“

Some travel memories are made at famous landmarks. Mine are sometimes made in random buildings with heating.

01

THE JOURNEY

A bridge to Sweden — after an hour of waiting

I landed in Copenhagen early in the morning and bought a train ticket across the world-famous Øresund Bridge. The plan was straightforward: visit Malmö in the morning, explore Copenhagen in the afternoon, and return home late that night.

So far, so good! I had narrowly missed the first train, but my ticket was valid for 90 minutes. I figured I could simply catch the next one. Little did I know that my journey to Sweden was about to become a test of patience.

The next scheduled train was cancelled, and the display board predicted heavy delays for the trains that followed. As it turned out, those predictions weren't exactly reassuring. The crowd on the platform grew larger while the list of cancellations and delays kept getting longer.

After losing more than an hour, I had spent quite some time pacing back and forth between the warm airport terminal and the freezing platform right outside it. Eventually, a suitable train finally arrived, and the journey to Sweden was quick and hassle-free from that point onwards.

Upon arriving in Malmö, I was greeted by a beautiful glass station building and a blast of cold air. An interesting combination, to say the least.

02

THE WEATHER SITUATION

I'm convinced the weather radar was lying

According to the weather radar, the temperature was just below freezing. To this day, I'm convinced that was a lie. There is no way the numbers alone could explain how unbelievably cold I felt.

I was wearing a winter jacket, a hood, a winter hat, gloves, and winter boots. In other words, I had dressed for winter. And yet, I was absolutely freezing. My limbs felt like blocks of ice, and simply walking around became an endurance test.

Naturally, I refused to let a little thing like the weather ruin my trip. I kept exploring the city, but every so often I had to find a warm public building or shop where I could thaw out before venturing back into the cold.

I rarely take such long breaks indoors when visiting a city. Usually, I want to make the most of my limited time and experience as much as possible. But Malmö was about to teach me that sometimes, survival takes priority over sightseeing.

03

THE UNEXPECTED HERO

Saved by a random corporate building

At some point, I stumbled upon what appeared to be a corporate complex. It had an entrance area next to a cafeteria, and most importantly, it was warm. At that moment, I couldn't have cared less about anything else.

I found a place to sit down and began warming up. There was just one small problem: it was lunchtime, and people kept walking up and down the nearby stairs. Sitting there without any obvious reason to be there felt a little awkward.

My strategy? Keep my eyes glued to my phone and try not to attract attention. I figured that if I looked busy enough, nobody would question why I was sitting in their entrance area like I had an important appointment with absolutely nobody.

And somehow, it worked! Nobody asked me to leave, so I stayed put until I had warmed up enough to face the outdoors again.

✦

To whoever owns that building: thank you for providing an unofficial warming station to one very cold and slightly desperate traveller.

Honestly, I rarely spend so much time indoors while travelling. But that day, a random corporate building became the unexpected hero of my Malmö adventure. It might not be listed in any travel guide, but it certainly deserves a special mention in mine.

04

EXPLORING MALMÖ

Lovely corners, industrial vibes, and a view of Denmark

Once I had regained enough feeling in my limbs, I continued exploring Malmö. The city had a few lovely parks, small sculptures, attractive buildings, and a great view of the Øresund Bridge connecting Sweden and Denmark.

At the same time, the city gave off somewhat industrial vibes. It wasn't unpleasant, but it didn't exactly sweep me off my feet either. Considering how much time I had spent trying not to freeze, perhaps I wasn't in the best position to judge its charm fairly.

All in all, Malmö was a decent enough introduction to Sweden, even if it wasn't quite the experience I had imagined.

At the end of my visit, I headed straight from Malmö Central Station to the centre of Copenhagen. I still had another city to explore before heading home that night, so there was no time to waste.

What happened next deserves a story of its own — especially because it involved a free chicken roll and some rather unfortunate consequences.

But more on that in my Copenhagen travel story!

MY FINAL THOUGHTS

Would I recommend Malmö?

Malmö had its lovely spots, attractive architecture, and a beautiful view of the Øresund Bridge. It was a decent introduction to Sweden, and I'm glad I got to experience another country on this spontaneous trip.

However, the freezing weather and the transport chaos certainly didn't make the best first impression. I spent a surprising amount of time searching for warmth instead of exploring, and the city itself didn't quite win me over.

Still, I wouldn't rule out giving Sweden another chance. A longer trip, better preparation, and temperatures that don't make me question the reliability of weather forecasts might lead to a very different experience.

✦
THE ONE DAY EXPLORER VERDICT

A decent introduction to Sweden, a few lovely sights, and one corporate building I'll forever be grateful to — even though I never learned its name.
