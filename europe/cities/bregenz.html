TRAVEL DIARY · AUSTRIA

Bregenz – Waited for Sun, Got Rain Instead

A day trip in search of sunshine and Lake Constance, featuring torrential rain, emergency chocolate shopping, photogenic swans and one rather awkward parking lot finale.

📍 Bregenz, Austria 🌧️ A rainy day trip ✍️ Personal travel story
01

THE STORY BEGINS

All I wanted was a little sunshine

I can still remember how I used to refuse to go on road trips because I was waiting for the perfect weather. My car adventures were usually spontaneous, and checking the forecast wasn't exactly a priority. But for Bregenz, I had a specific vision: clear skies, warm temperatures and a beautiful day by Lake Constance. After all, the lake stretches between Germany, Austria and Switzerland, and I wanted to experience it at its best. Naturally, things turned out rather differently.

“

I came looking for sunshine. The weather had other plans.

01

THE ARRIVAL

A parking spot and a very unwelcome surprise

Arriving from Germany, I managed to squeeze my car into a rather tight parking spot in an open lot near the train station. At least it was inexpensive, so I considered that a small victory. The weather, however, was not cooperating. It was cold, and sunshine was nowhere to be seen.

Fortunately, I had a jacket somewhere in the trunk of my car. A small but crucial discovery! With that sorted, I was ready to explore Bregenz, even if the temperature was doing its best to discourage me.

I headed towards the town centre and made my way through the old medieval part of the city. What surprised me most was how quiet it was. I wandered through the historic alleyways, surrounded by traditional buildings, without encountering a single soul.

It felt as though I had somehow booked a private tour of Bregenz without paying a single cent for it. No crowds, no queues and nobody getting in the way of my photos. The city was practically mine!

02

THE WEATHER STRIKES BACK

Thirty minutes of rain and one emergency chocolate bar

My peaceful exploration eventually led me to a somewhat newer part of town. And there, the weather decided to remind me who was really in charge. Rain! Not a few harmless drops, not a little drizzle that would pass in five minutes, but proper, relentless rain.

It absolutely poured for at least half an hour. Along with plenty of other visitors and locals, I had no choice but to find somewhere to hide. My shelter of choice? The entrance to a branch of Sparkasse. Not exactly the scenic experience I had imagined when planning my trip to Bregenz.

Eventually, I got tired of standing around, watching the rain and waiting for my day to improve. I decided to make a run for it across the rain-soaked main square and take refuge in a supermarket on the other side.

I hadn't planned on buying anything. I was simply looking for somewhere dry to spend a little time. But after wandering through the aisles for what felt like an eternity, I emerged with a chocolate bar in my hand. Apparently, surviving the weather required a snack. Who was I to argue?

✦

Original plan: enjoy the sunshine. Actual plan: shelter under a bank roof and accidentally go grocery shopping.

Thankfully, the rain eventually eased off. I could finally continue exploring without having to calculate the distance between me and the nearest roof every few seconds.

03

THE MAIN EVENT

Lake Constance, a floating stage and some very photogenic swans

With the town centre explored, it was finally time to head towards Lake Constance. This was, after all, the main reason I had come to Bregenz. The lake was still cold and the surroundings were wet, but by that point I had adjusted my expectations considerably.

As long as the sky didn't open up again, I was happy. Sometimes you have to lower your standards a little to enjoy a day out!

One of the sights I wanted to see was the famous floating stage associated with the Bregenz Festival. The festival is well known for its spectacular productions on the lake, and seeing the stage in person was a highlight of my visit.

I also made my way as close to the water as I could. The only problem was that the water levels had risen enough to flood the piers. I wasn't going to let a little water ruin the experience, though. After everything the weather had put me through already, this was hardly going to stop me!

And then came the unexpected stars of the day: the swans. Thanks to the high water levels, they were lounging along the shore, looking completely unbothered by the conditions. They posed beautifully for my photo collection, apparently understanding the assignment better than the weather forecast had.

In the end, those elegant birds turned out to be one of the highlights of my trip. Who needs perfect sunshine when you have a flock of photogenic swans?

04

THE PARKING LOT FINALE

Ten extra minutes and a public parking performance

After finishing my exploration, I headed back towards the train station and my car. But when I noticed that I still had ten minutes left on my parking ticket, I decided to have another little look around the area.

Should I admit what happened next? Probably not. Let's just say that those ten minutes somehow turned into a little more than ten minutes, meaning my parking was no longer entirely legal. Oops.

To make myself feel slightly better, I noticed that I wasn't the only one who had overstayed. Quite a few other cars were still parked there with expired tickets. I'm not saying that makes it acceptable, of course. I'm simply pointing out that I wasn't alone in my questionable time-management skills.

But the real challenge wasn't the parking ticket. It was getting my car out of the parking spot. By then, the day had progressed, and the lot had transformed from the relatively quiet place I had arrived at that morning into a crowded, chaotic scene.

The moment other drivers realised I was preparing to leave, they started waiting for me to pull out. And as soon as my car was clear, they were ready to swoop in and claim the newly available spot. Suddenly, my departure had become a public event.

I have always found it awkward to manoeuvre into or out of a parking space while other drivers sit nearby, patiently watching and waiting for me to finish. It makes me feel as though I'm performing in front of an audience, except there are no tickets, no applause and absolutely no room for mistakes.

Naturally, that did wonders for my confidence. Nothing says a relaxing end to a day trip quite like trying to reverse out of a parking space while strangers silently observe your every move.

In the end, I was probably more embarrassed than the driver waiting to take my spot. But hey, at least I had my pick of spaces when I arrived that morning!

✦

The parking spot was cheap. The rain was free. The audience for my exit manoeuvre? Apparently included.

05

LOOKING BACK

Sometimes the weather just refuses to cooperate

Despite the cold, the downpour and the slightly uncomfortable parking lot finale, I had a fantastic day in Bregenz. I explored the old town, discovered the famous floating stage, enjoyed the lakeside scenery and came away with a lovely collection of swan photos.

Bregenz is the capital of Vorarlberg and is well worth a visit. Between its historic streets, cultural attractions and location on Lake Constance, there is plenty to enjoy, even when the weather doesn't exactly match the brochure.

Looking back, I also find it amusing that I used to avoid road trips whenever the forecast wasn't perfect. These days, I know that a trip can still be worthwhile even when the sunshine never shows up. You might get rained on, end up sheltering outside a bank or buy chocolate you never intended to buy. But you can still have a wonderful day.

So, would I recommend Bregenz? Absolutely. Just don't make the same mistake I did and assume that waiting for perfect weather guarantees anything. Sometimes, you simply have to go and make the best of whatever the day brings.

MY FINAL THOUGHTS

Would I recommend Bregenz?

Definitely! Bregenz is a lovely destination for a day trip, especially if you enjoy historic town centres, lakeside walks and beautiful scenery. The floating stage and Lake Constance are well worth seeing, and the old town has plenty of charm.

Just remember that the weather might have other ideas. Bring a jacket, check the forecast if you feel like being sensible, and don't be surprised if your sunny lakeside adventure turns into a search for shelter and an unplanned chocolate run.

✦
THE ONE DAY EXPLORER VERDICT

A charming town, a beautiful lake, surprisingly photogenic swans and absolutely no guarantee of sunshine. Bregenz is worth the trip, even when the weather refuses to play along.
