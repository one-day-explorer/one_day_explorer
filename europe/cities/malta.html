TRAVEL DIARY · MALTA

Malta – A Day Trip That Took Longer Than Planned

Five destinations, less than 30 minutes of sleep, two airport nights and one very long journey home. What was supposed to be a simple day trip turned into an adventure I won't forget anytime soon.

📍 Malta ☀️ January 2026 ✍️ Personal travel story
07

THE STORY BEGINS

A spontaneous trip with almost no sleep

In mid-January 2026, I spontaneously decided to visit Malta. I booked my flights just a few days in advance and, for the first time, dared to land in a foreign country at night without having any accommodation arranged. Looking back, that turned out to be the biggest mistake of the entire trip.

What followed was a day spent exploring five different places across Malta, a night on an airport floor, an unexpected flight delay and a missed night train. Somehow, a trip that was supposed to last just one day became a test of my patience, energy and ability to function on almost no sleep.

“

The original plan was a day trip to Malta. The actual plan apparently included an airport sleepover and a second one back home.

01

THE FIRST AIRPORT NIGHT

Arriving without a place to sleep

I wouldn't recommend arriving at Malta International Airport in the middle of the night without having somewhere to stay. Although the airport remains open 24 hours a day, after midnight it felt as though the entire place had emptied out.

Airport cleaning operations were underway, and I spent what felt like an eternity wandering around the arrivals hall. I couldn't help feeling as though the staff were staring at me, wondering why I was simply hanging around instead of leaving.

In retrospect, though, I probably felt far more awkward about my presence than the airport staff actually did.

In any case, I couldn't — and didn't want to — leave the airport. I had no accommodation booked, and I certainly wasn't going to spend money looking for somewhere to stay at one in the morning just to avoid an uncomfortable night.

After wandering around for ages and trying to stay out of everyone's way, I eventually found what seemed to be a suitable spot to get some rest.

Well, that was the theory, anyway.

I suspect my improvised sleeping spot wasn't entirely permitted, and it was certainly nowhere near comfortable. It was just a dirty, freezing-cold stretch of unheated airport floor tucked away in a corner. Luxury accommodation it was not.

Somehow, I survived the night with less than 30 minutes of sleep. At six in the morning, I finally left the airport and headed towards Valletta, Malta's capital.

✦

Accommodation costs: saved. Sleep quality: practically nonexistent. Holiday experience: already questionable.

02

VALLETTA AT SUNRISE

A beautiful city and a very tired traveller

I arrived in Valletta just as the sun was rising and began walking from the bus station towards the city centre. Despite the lack of sleep, I was immediately impressed by the city.

Valletta is genuinely worth visiting. Its historic buildings, narrow streets and Mediterranean atmosphere make it a wonderful place to explore on foot. Around every corner, there seemed to be another beautiful building or another picturesque street waiting to be discovered.

Unfortunately, my body was beginning to remind me that spending an entire night on a freezing airport floor wasn't exactly a brilliant idea.

At one point, I found a park bench near the Central Bank of Malta and decided to lie down for a while in the hope of catching up on some sleep.

I managed a quick power nap, although nothing remotely resembling a proper night's rest. Still, even that brief moment of sleep made a difference. I had just enough energy to get back on my feet and continue exploring.

Another surprise was the temperature. Despite wearing my winter jacket, I felt incredibly cold. The forecast had predicted temperatures between 15 and 20°C, so I hadn't expected to struggle with the cold quite so much.

Thankfully, I had brought plenty of warm clothing. With a little rest behind me, I continued wandering through Valletta's city centre and eventually stumbled upon Floriana completely by chance.

03

AN UNEXPECTED DETOUR

Floriana, a little ferry and a change of scenery

Floriana lies just south of Valletta and won me over mainly with its small green parks. There wasn't a huge amount else to discover during my visit, but the greenery provided a pleasant change from the historic streets of the capital.

After a little exploration, I made my way towards the ferry terminal. There, I did something I had never done before on any of my travels: I took a tourist ferry.

And honestly, it was a pretty good decision.

The crossing to Sliema took just 15 minutes and offered a completely different perspective on the area. Sometimes, even a short boat ride can make a destination feel like a whole new adventure.

After spending the morning wandering around Valletta and Floriana, I was ready to discover another side of Malta.

04

ALONG THE COAST

From Sliema to San Ġiljan

Sliema felt completely different from Valletta. Instead of mainly historic architecture and narrow streets, I found modern buildings, countless shops and a long seaside promenade.

And I don't mean that in a negative way! Sliema had its own character, and the waterfront was particularly lovely. Since the city lies directly opposite Valletta, separated by the Mediterranean, there were also fantastic views of the capital across the water.

I decided to walk the entire length of Sliema along the seaside promenade. It was a beautiful stretch to explore, and a welcome opportunity to enjoy Malta's coastline.

Eventually, my walk brought me to San Ġiljan, just beyond Sliema. Compared with the places I had visited earlier that day, it felt livelier.

However, by that point, I had already seen quite a lot of similar architecture, so the change of scenery wasn't quite as exciting as I might have hoped.

I had actually continued walking there for two rather simple reasons: I recognised the town's name, and I had just received an email from my airline.

My return flight, which had originally been scheduled for the late afternoon, would now depart no earlier than seven in the evening.

At the time, I didn't think too much of it. A few extra hours in Malta didn't sound particularly terrible. Little did I know that this delay would be the beginning of the real nightmare.

05

THE JOURNEY HOME

A delayed flight, a missing train and another airport night

There was one important detail that made the flight delay particularly inconvenient: I live more than two hours away from my home country's international airport. Getting home therefore meant catching a long-distance train after landing.

With my flight now departing much later in the evening, I knew I would arrive home very late. Fortunately, there was one final night train that seemed to offer a glimmer of hope.

If everything went according to plan, I could still make it home that night.

Of course, everything did not go according to plan.

I waited at the airport train station, watching and waiting for the last train that could get me home. But the train simply never arrived.

With no practical way to complete the journey that night, I was left with an unfortunate conclusion: I would have to spend another night at an airport.

This time, at least, I was at my home airport — and I have to say that it was a considerable improvement over Malta.

The landside area had sleeping pods and noticeably more seating. Malta's airport, by comparison, had offered absolutely nowhere to sit comfortably, at least outside the security area.

Half-exhausted and thoroughly annoyed, I did my best to make it through the second airport night. At four in the morning, I finally boarded the first available train home.

By the time I reached home, I was completely done. The extreme lack of sleep had worn down my patience, and all I wanted was to get into my own bed and sleep properly.

So that's exactly what I did. After everything that had happened, collapsing into bed was easily one of the best moments of the entire trip.

MY FINAL THOUGHTS

Would I recommend Malta?

Absolutely! Despite the lack of sleep, the freezing airport floor, the flight delay and the missing night train, I genuinely think Malta is a fantastic destination.

Valletta's historic architecture, Floriana's little green parks, Sliema's seaside promenade and the livelier atmosphere of San Ġiljan made for a varied day of exploring. Malta has beautiful coastlines, impressive architecture and plenty of places worth discovering.

My biggest lesson? Book your accommodation before arriving, even if you're only planning a short visit. And when travelling home, don't underestimate the importance of checking the connections between your flight and your final train.

Would I do it all the same way again? Probably not. Was Malta worth visiting? Definitely.

✦
THE ONE DAY EXPLORER VERDICT

Beautiful coastal views, historic streets and five destinations in one exhausting day. Malta is absolutely worth visiting — just arrange somewhere to sleep before you arrive, and make sure that final train actually exists!
