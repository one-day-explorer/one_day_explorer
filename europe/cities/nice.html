TRAVEL DIARY · FRANCE

Nice – Stronghold of Police Presence

A spontaneous five-day getaway, a first Uber ride that went spectacularly wrong, and a football match I never saw coming.

📍 Nice, France ☀️ Five-day trip ✍️ Personal travel story
01

THE STORY BEGINS

A last-minute trip with an unexpected twist

Some trips are planned months in advance. Others come together at the last minute and turn into adventures you never could have planned. My five days in Nice definitely fell into the latter category. From a chaotic late-night arrival to an unexpected encounter with football hooligans, this trip gave me a story I certainly wasn't expecting to tell.

“

Sometimes, the best travel stories begin when nothing goes according to plan.

01

THE ARRIVAL

A spontaneous trip with a bumpy start

The idea of spending five days in Nice came about rather spontaneously. I booked my flights and accommodation just two days in advance, and less than 48 hours later, I was already in France. Things didn't exactly go smoothly, though.

It all started when my flight, which was already scheduled to depart from my home airport in the evening, was delayed even further. I ended up spending more time at the departure airport than planned. That wasn't too bad in itself, but it meant that by the time I arrived in Nice, the trains to my accommodation had stopped running. I had no choice but to rely on a ride service instead.

02

THE UBER ADVENTURE

From a rusty clunker to a Mercedes

That was the first time in my life I'd ever used Uber. To make matters worse, I couldn't even find my first driver. Standing outside late at night, phone in hand, I scrambled to locate the right car, checking the license plate to make sure I had the correct vehicle. The Uber hadn't arrived where the app said it would.

Just as I finally spotted the right car, it drove off right in front of me. I'd simply taken too long to find it. Brilliant start, right?

A short while later, however, Uber sent another driver, and that was definitely an upgrade! While the first car had been little more than a rusty clunker plastered with a giant Uber logo, the second was a sleek Mercedes C-Class sedan with tinted windows. This driver was much easier to spot, and I could finally relax as he whisked me away to my accommodation.

He was even kind enough to wait outside after dropping me off, making sure I got safely inside the building. Needless to say, he earned himself a tip.

✦

My first-ever Uber ride: a complete disaster. The second one: unexpectedly luxurious.

As for my accommodation, I stayed in a student residence with a self-check-in system. To this day, even after all the trips I've taken since, it remains the best place I've ever stayed.

03

EXPLORING THE CITY

Mediterranean charm and historic streets

The next morning, I headed out early to a supermarket a little further away. There was actually a massive store right beneath my apartment, but its opening hours were completely impractical. I didn't end up visiting it until my very last evening.

I didn't actually explore Nice that day, either. Instead, I waited until the following afternoon. In hindsight, that wasn't the smartest move, although I only realised that after the trip was over.

Regardless, Nice is a truly magnificent city that's well worth visiting. Situated on the Mediterranean coast, it boasts a long seafront promenade that's perfect for a leisurely stroll. With beautiful beaches and beach clubs, it's easy to see why the city appeals to sun-seekers.

If urban exploration is more your thing, you'll find plenty to discover in the historic Old Town, with its narrow streets, old buildings, churches and monasteries. Food lovers won't be disappointed either, thanks to the wide variety of restaurants and pubs.

And that's not even mentioning the many parks or the grand main square, with its distinctive chequerboard-patterned ground!

04

THE UNEXPECTED TWIST

A city under heavy police presence

One thing that kept catching my attention, however, was the sheer number of police officers throughout the city. I'm not talking about ordinary uniformed officers, either, but members of the Gendarmerie wearing balaclavas and carrying firearms. Even the military was out and about, keeping a relatively low profile.

One encounter that has stuck in my memory to this day took place on the seafront promenade. A large crowd, accompanied by a police escort, was heading straight towards me. To be precise, they were clearly football fans wearing their team jerseys, and they didn't exactly look like the most peaceful bunch.

The streets were also clogged with countless cars, all seemingly heading for the same destination: the stadium of the local football team, OGC Nice.

05

THE REVEAL

Football, rivalry and a high-risk match

At the time, I had no idea what was going on or why such a massive security operation was underway. A quick Google search, however, cleared things up almost immediately.

I later learned that OGC Nice were playing AS Roma in the Europa League that day. The match had reportedly been classified as high-risk, prompting a large police deployment to prevent potential violence between the two sets of fans.

Suddenly, the masked officers, the military presence and the crowds of football fans all made a lot more sense!

MY FINAL THOUGHTS

Would I recommend Nice?

Despite everything, I felt safe throughout my time there, regardless of whether the police seemed to be lurking around every corner. Perhaps doing a quick internet search before my trips would be a sensible idea after all!

Still, these unexpected situations always leave me with a juicy story to tell, and Nice is absolutely worth a visit. Whether you're a culture enthusiast, a beach lover or a football fanatic, there's something for everyone.

✦
THE ONE DAY EXPLORER VERDICT

Mediterranean beauty, fascinating streets and an unexpected football adventure. Nice definitely left its mark on me.
