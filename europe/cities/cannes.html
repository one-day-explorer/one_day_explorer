TRAVEL DIARY · FRANCE

Cannes – Luxury Shopping and Disturbing the Peace of the Dead

Film festival fame, luxury boutiques and an unexpectedly awkward encounter that made me rethink my sightseeing plans.

📍 Cannes, France ☀️ A morning on the Riviera ✍️ Personal travel story
02

THE STORY BEGINS

Luxury, film stars and an awkward surprise

Cannes was one of the stops during my five-day trip along the Côte d’Azur, which also took me through the south of France and Monaco. Known around the world for its famous film festival, the city had certainly caught my attention, although I arrived without any particular expectations.

What followed was a pleasant walk through a beautiful seaside town, a look at the world of luxury shopping and one rather uncomfortable encounter I definitely hadn't planned for.

“

I came for the famous film festival, discovered a paradise for luxury shoppers and accidentally interrupted a funeral. Just another travel day!

01

THE UNEXPECTED ENCOUNTER

A castle, a famous sign and a funeral

Before exploring the city centre, I headed up a hill on the eastern side of Cannes to see the famous city sign and the nearby castle. It seemed like a good place to start my visit, offering a chance to take a few photos and enjoy a different perspective of the town.

What I hadn't realised was that this historic building, which is open to the public, also serves as a venue for funeral services. And naturally, I managed to arrive at exactly the wrong moment.

While I was standing in the forecourt taking photos, a group of mourners emerged from the castle entrance to say their final goodbyes to a loved one. A coffin was resting in the back of a hearse parked nearby, and it quickly became clear that I had stumbled into a very private and emotional moment.

I hadn't gone inside the castle, but even standing outside suddenly felt rather awkward. I certainly hadn't intended to intrude on anyone's farewell, so I decided not to linger.

Instead, I took a different route back down towards the town, winding through narrow alleyways and leaving the unexpected scene behind me.

✦

Note to self: before taking sightseeing photos, make sure the location isn't hosting a funeral.

02

EXPLORING CANNES

Old streets, sailboats and the world of cinema

After that slightly uncomfortable start, I continued exploring Cannes on foot. I wandered through the old town and along the harbour, where countless sailboats and yachts filled the water.

Eventually, I made my way to the building that hosts the annual Cannes Film Festival. Given the city's international reputation, this was one of the places I particularly wanted to see.

To be honest, the architecture itself wasn't especially spectacular. The building was nevertheless covered in film posters, leaving little doubt about what Cannes is most proud of. Even without a particularly impressive exterior, its connection to the world of cinema was impossible to miss.

Although I only spent a morning in Cannes, the walk gave me a good impression of the town. Between the historic streets, the harbour and its famous film festival venue, there was plenty to see without having to follow a strict sightseeing plan.

03

THE LUXURY SIDE

Endless boutiques and countless ways to spend money

On my way back, I decided not to retrace my steps along the harbour. Instead, I headed further into the city centre, where I stumbled upon an entirely different side of Cannes.

The streets were lined with luxury boutiques, from high-end fashion labels to perfume houses and watchmakers. Wherever I looked, another shop seemed to be waiting to tempt passers-by with something expensive.

In other words, there were endless opportunities to part with your money!

Fortunately, I managed to resist the temptation. I'm not much of a shopper to begin with, so browsing the boutiques was enough for me. I didn't need to bring home a designer handbag or an expensive watch to complete my visit.

That said, if luxury shopping is your thing, Cannes is definitely worth adding to your itinerary. The selection of boutiques alone makes it an appealing stop for anyone who enjoys browsing high-end brands.

✦

My wallet survived Cannes. For luxury shopping enthusiasts, however, that might be a very different story.

MY FINAL THOUGHTS

Would I recommend Cannes?

My visit to Cannes was fairly straightforward, apart from the unexpected encounter at the castle. I spent the morning exploring the town, admiring the harbour and checking out the famous film festival venue before discovering its luxurious shopping streets.

There wasn't a dramatic adventure around every corner, but not every destination needs to deliver a major plot twist. Cannes is a wonderful seaside town with a distinctive atmosphere, and it makes for a worthwhile stop when exploring the French Riviera.

Whether you're interested in cinema, enjoy wandering through historic streets or love luxury shopping, there's something here for you. Just be a little more mindful of your surroundings than I was when visiting the castle!

✦
THE ONE DAY EXPLORER VERDICT

A charming seaside town, a glimpse into the world of cinema and luxury shopping galore — with one unexpectedly awkward moment along the way. Cannes is definitely worth a visit.
