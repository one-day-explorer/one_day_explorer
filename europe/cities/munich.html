TRAVEL DIARY · GERMANY

Munich – A Mad Dash Through the City Center

A spontaneous road trip, a parking space that no longer existed, and an expensive parking garage that nearly became the main attraction of the day.

📍 Munich, Germany 🗓️ 2024 ✍️ Personal travel story
01

THE STORY BEGINS

A city trip that turned into a parking nightmare

Some spontaneous trips turn into unforgettable adventures for all the right reasons. Others teach you valuable lessons about planning ahead, managing your time, and never underestimating the cost of parking. My 2024 visit to Munich was definitely one of the latter.

After spending longer than expected in Regensburg earlier that same day, I arrived in Bavaria's largest city already running short on time. Then the parking space I had picked out in advance turned out to be completely inaccessible. What followed was a frustrating search that consumed far more of my day than I had ever intended.

“

I wasn't about to drive all the way to Munich just to turn around and go home. Unfortunately, finding somewhere to park turned out to be an adventure of its own.

01

THE ROAD TRIP

Regensburg first, Munich second

Munich, the largest city in Bavaria, seemed like a place worth visiting. So, in 2024, I decided to take a chance and spontaneously drove there. As I do with all my road trips, I checked out parking options in advance — and then I was off.

My journey had actually started with another stop: Regensburg. But that's a story for another day. As it turned out, I ended up spending longer there than planned, which left me a little pressed for time by the time I continued to Munich.

Still, I wasn't going to skip the city altogether. I had made the journey, and I wanted to see at least a little of what Munich had to offer.

I had even done my homework and looked for a suitable parking space beforehand. For once, I thought I had everything under control.

02

THE PARKING DILEMMA

The parking space that didn't exist anymore

There was just one problem: the parking space I had selected in advance was no longer an option. The entire area had been cordoned off.

So there I was, driving around Bavaria's largest city, desperately trying to find somewhere else to leave my car. My original plan had disappeared, and I was now running out of time.

I searched and searched, but many parking areas were already full. Some of the spaces that were occupied didn't exactly seem to be used efficiently, either — which was particularly frustrating when every available spot counted.

But there was no way I was going to give up and head home without seeing Munich. I just had to find somewhere to park. Surely, that couldn't be too difficult in a city this size?

✦

My original parking plan: gone. My time to explore: disappearing. My determination to find a spot: still very much alive.

03

THE CITY TRAFFIC

Red lights, traffic jams and driving in circles

The search quickly became an experience in itself. I battled my way through heavy city traffic, sat at countless red lights, got stuck in traffic jams, drove around in circles and kept looking for any available parking space.

Every minute spent behind the wheel was another minute I wouldn't be spending exploring the city. And with my earlier stop in Regensburg having already eaten into my schedule, I really didn't have much time to spare.

Eventually, after all that driving around, I finally found a parking garage.

You might think this was the moment when everything finally worked out. Well, not quite.

The garage was so far from the city center that the walk there and back became a significant part of my visit. After all the time I had spent searching for parking, I now had to spend even more time just getting to the place I had come to see.

✦

At least I had finally found a parking space. The distance from the city center was a detail I would have to deal with later.

04

FINALLY IN MUNICH

Too many people, too little time

Once I finally made it into the city center, I was ready to explore. Unfortunately, by that point, my patience was already wearing thin.

Munich itself didn't exactly blow me away. There were simply too many people packed into one place: tourists, street vendors, and some event I knew absolutely nothing about.

Perhaps the crowds wouldn't have bothered me as much on another day. But after the traffic, the parking chaos and the long walk into the city, I had reached the point where I had simply had enough.

I spent only a short time in Munich before deciding to head back to my car. It felt a little ridiculous to have made the effort to get there only to leave again so soon, but I wasn't enjoying myself enough to justify staying any longer.

And, of course, I still had that long walk back to the parking garage ahead of me.

05

THE EXPENSIVE SURPRISE

That parking bill hurt

Just when I thought the day couldn't become any more frustrating, I reached the parking garage and was confronted with the final unpleasant surprise: the parking bill.

I honestly don't remember the exact amount anymore, but I do remember that it felt like an absolute fortune.

And what made it even worse was the fact that the garage was so far away from the city center. I had spent ages searching for a space, then more time walking to and from the center, and had barely spent any time actually enjoying Munich itself.

In other words, I had managed to spend a considerable amount of time dealing with parking — only to pay a ridiculous amount for the privilege.

I don't know what I had expected that day, but it certainly wasn't to spend more time trekking between a parking garage and the city center than exploring the city itself.

✦

The parking space was no longer free, the walk was longer than expected, and the sightseeing was shorter than planned. What a bargain.

06

LOOKING BACK

Was Munich really the problem?

To be fair, I don't want to badmouth Munich. It is a huge city with countless residents and tourists, and busy streets and crowded areas are hardly surprising.

The truth is that I simply didn't see enough of the city to form a fair overall opinion. Too much of my visit was spent dealing with traffic, searching for parking and trying to make up for lost time.

It's also worth remembering that this was one of my earlier road trips, back when I was still a little inexperienced when it came to travelling. I have learned a lot since then, and I would probably approach a trip like this differently today.

Looking back, I might have enjoyed Munich more if I had allowed myself more time or chosen a different way of getting there. Instead, the parking situation ended up defining much of my experience.

Sometimes, the destination isn't the problem. Sometimes, you just don't give it a fair chance because everything that happens along the way has already tested your patience.

MY FINAL THOUGHTS

Would I visit Munich again?

Maybe. I wouldn't rule it out completely, because I know I didn't see enough of the city to judge it properly. There is a good chance that a more relaxed visit would give me a very different impression.

But if I do return, I will seriously consider taking the train. That way, I can avoid the city traffic, forget about searching for parking and spend more of my time actually exploring.

Do I have any concrete plans to visit again? Perhaps — but for now, probably not.

Munich, maybe we'll meet again one day. Just don't expect me to bring a car.

✦
THE ONE DAY EXPLORER VERDICT

A city I didn't get to experience properly, a parking space that vanished, and a parking bill that left a lasting impression. Next time, the train gets my vote.
